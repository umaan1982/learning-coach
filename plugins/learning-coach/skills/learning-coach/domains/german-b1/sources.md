# Sources: German B1

Use these for grounding corrections, building cards and finding input material. Official sources come first.

## Official (Goethe-Institut)
- **Goethe-Zertifikat B1 Wortliste**: the ~2,400 lexical units the exam is based on, sorted alphabetically with example sentences, plus thematic word groups. The basis for vocabulary cards. https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/da/Goethe-Zertifikat_B1_Wortliste.pdf
- **Übungsmaterialien / Modellsätze B1** (free practice sets with audio and answer keys): find them on the Goethe-Institut page for the Goethe-Zertifikat B1 under "Übungsmaterialien".
- **Durchführungsbestimmungen B1** (exam rules): https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/pt/Durchfuehrungsbestimmungen_B1.pdf

## Input: reading
- **nachrichtenleicht.de** (Deutschlandfunk): weekly news in simple German, A2–B1.
- **DW Learn German** (learngerman.dw.com): graded courses including *Nicos Weg* (A1–B1, video + exercises) and *Top-Thema* (B1 articles with audio and vocabulary).
- Graded readers (e.g. Klett/Hueber/Cornelsen *Lektüren* at A2/B1).

## Input: listening
- **DW "Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten"**: daily news read slowly, with transcript. Good for the metacognitive listening cycle and shadowing.
- **Easy German** (YouTube/podcast): street interviews with subtitles. Natural speed.
- **Slow German** (podcast, Annik Rubens): slow monologues on everyday topics.

## Reference grammar & dictionaries
- **DWDS** (dwds.de): dictionary with corpus examples, frequency and collocations. Good for checking usage.
- **Duden online** (duden.de): spelling, gender, plural.
- **dict.cc / LEO**: bilingual lookups.
- **verbformen.de**: conjugation tables (Präteritum, Partizip, auxiliary).
- Reference grammar for edge cases: e.g. Dreyer/Schmitt *Lehr- und Übungsbuch der deutschen Grammatik* or *Grammatik aktiv A1–B1* (Cornelsen).

## Exam training books (optional)
- *Mit Erfolg zum Goethe-Zertifikat B1* (Klett), *Fit fürs Goethe-Zertifikat B1* (Hueber): additional full practice tests.
